# Art direction

Read the project's saved design and approved references. Identify the audience, primary action and existing identity constraints. For a new design with meaningful choices, make two or three working first sections that materially differ in composition, type hierarchy or scene staging. Label each with its central decision and one tradeoff. Keep a failed preview associated with its own direction.

Record the selected direction, who selected it, why it fits, reference and asset IDs, and explicit open questions in the project direction. When adjusting an existing design, name the affected region and preserve the decisions outside it. Pass the selected revision to Code and QA. Save a screenshot of the implemented first section at wide and narrow widths.
