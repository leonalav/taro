# 3D construction

For each scene store object and asset IDs with exact revisions, transforms and units, named camera poses, light direction and intensity, material variants and fallback information in the design handoff. Read DesignRecipe details where available and adapt the relevant mechanism to incumbent assets and styles. Record the example version and settings if you use it.

Expose HTML targets for the same choices as drag or pick input. Keep a poster and descriptions available before loading and after renderer failure. Budget pixel ratio and geometry or point density, reuse GPU resources, render resting scenes on demand, and pause loops when the scene leaves view.

Save named checkpoint captures and interaction results for the actual scene and fallback. Include viewport, quality tier, reduced-motion setting, frame samples when measured, and unresolved hardware or model constraints.
