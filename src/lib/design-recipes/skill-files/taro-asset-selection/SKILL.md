# Asset selection

For each placement, record subject, role, fidelity needs, focal point or camera target, desktop and phone framing, and the fallback. Record the asset ID and revision, source, creator, rights status and transformation inputs in the project asset collection. Preserve the original alongside any crop, compressed asset or poster. Mark generated artwork and unverified rights in the record.

Check the actual delivery rendition in the browser. Save proof of the mobile crop or model state and the loading or missing-asset behavior. Mark placeholders distinctly from approved assets and show unresolved rights in the handoff.
